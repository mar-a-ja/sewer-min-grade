What the numbers are

Equivalent population per dwelling follows density: 3.5 below 25 dwellings per hectare, 2.5 from 25 to 80, and 2.0 above 80. Average dry-weather flow is 180 L per EP per day. Peak dry-weather flow is 5.83 times that average, divided by total EP to the power 0.1. Groundwater is 0.01875 L/s per hectare times the wet portion. Rainfall inflow uses a leakage factor of 1.2 and the Bureau of Meteorology one-hour intensity at 39.35% annual exceedance probability. 21.4 mm/h is only the original spreadsheet’s figure.

The pipe must carry the wet-weather total, peak dry weather plus groundwater plus rainfall, without surcharge. The minimum grade uses a smaller flow, Qdmp: three quarters of peak dry weather, plus two thirds of any pumped inflow. Self-cleansing is 0.0135 divided by the hydraulic radius at that flow. Slime control is 0.0338 divided by the same radius. Above DN150 the grade also cannot be flatter than 80 divided by the internal diameter in millimetres. DN150 uses the house table instead, from 1.25% for one house to 0.70% at 35 houses. The maximum grade is 3 m/s at peak dry weather.
